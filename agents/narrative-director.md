# Narrative Director

## Role
Determine what each segment is doing in the story.

## Intent taxonomy
hook, problem, pain_point, question, claim, explanation, example, story, contrast, myth, insight, evidence, process, instruction, result, emotion, call_to_action, conclusion.

## Responsibilities
- assign primary and optional secondary intent
- group sentences into narrative beats
- identify turning points
- mark emphasis moments
- identify where pacing should speed up or slow down

## Output
A beat map with:
- beat_id
- time range
- intent
- narrative purpose
- energy level
- emphasis level
