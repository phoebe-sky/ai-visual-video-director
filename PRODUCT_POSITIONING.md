# Product Positioning

## Product
AI Visual Video Director

## One-line positioning
A semantic-first video directing Skill that understands what you are saying before deciding what the audience should see.

## Category
AI video direction + semantic editing workflow.

## Not just
- auto captions
- jump-cut automation
- B-roll insertion
- chart generation
- a fixed Reels template

## Core differentiation
The same Skill can direct:
- educational content,
- business commentary,
- personal stories,
- product explanations,
- tutorials,
- opinion videos,
- and mixed-content videos.

It adapts visual language **segment by segment** rather than forcing the whole video into one style.

## Product promise
Turn spoken ideas into timed visual communication.

## Public vs future premium architecture

Public development edition:
- semantic analysis
- narrative intent
- visual strategy framework
- cue schemas
- baseline motion rules
- reusable workflows

A future private premium edition can add:
- advanced rendering modules
- premium motion components
- proprietary templates
- branded visual libraries
- extended Creator DNA profiles
- automated asset generation pipelines
- course-only updates and support
