# Sample Edit Plan

## Content profile
Primary: educational / opinion
Secondary: personal experience
Visual density: medium

## Beat 1 — Myth
Presenter + kinetic typography.
Do not cut away from the speaker.

## Beat 2 — Insight
Add a three-node diagram:
Human Judgment → Workflow → AI

## Beat 3 — Time comparison
Show:
3 HR → 30 MIN

Use comparison + counter.
Do not create a bar chart unless the design system requires one.

## QC
- keep subtitles below diagram
- do not cover face
- no invented efficiency percentage unless stated or mathematically derived and explicitly labeled
