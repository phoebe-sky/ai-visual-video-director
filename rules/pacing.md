# Pacing Rules

- Hooks: faster visual response, shorter delay
- Explanations: hold visuals long enough to comprehend
- Stories: allow pauses and presenter-led moments
- Comparisons: synchronize both sides clearly
- Processes: reveal one step at a time
- Conclusions: reduce clutter and increase message clarity
- CTA: one dominant action, not multiple competing elements

Cutting pace and visual pace are separate. A fast-cut video can still use restrained overlays.
