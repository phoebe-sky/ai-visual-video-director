# Narrative Intent Rules

Classify by function, not keywords alone.

- **hook**: earns attention immediately
- **problem / pain_point**: states friction, cost, risk or frustration
- **question**: creates curiosity or asks the viewer to consider something
- **claim**: asserts a position
- **explanation**: clarifies how or why
- **example**: demonstrates a concept
- **story**: describes lived or chronological experience
- **contrast**: compares alternatives, before/after, old/new
- **myth**: identifies a misconception
- **insight**: reframes the topic
- **evidence**: supports a claim with facts, data, proof or examples
- **process / instruction**: describes steps or actions
- **result**: shows outcome or impact
- **emotion**: conveys feeling as the primary narrative function
- **call_to_action**: asks the viewer to act
- **conclusion**: closes or summarizes

When multiple intents apply, choose one primary intent and at most two secondary intents.
