# Visual Density Rules

## Low
3–5 major visual events per 30 seconds.
Use for interviews, emotional stories, premium professional tone.

## Medium
5–9 major visual events per 30 seconds.
Default for knowledge and creator content.

## High
9–15 major visual events per 30 seconds.
Use only for dense short-form explainers.

## Guardrails
- no major visual change every sentence by default
- allow quiet sections
- do not exceed creator profile
- reduce density when the speaker's facial expression or gesture carries meaning
