# Motion Selection Rules

Use motion to explain or emphasize.

- numeric change → counter, scale, directional motion
- relationships/process → line_draw
- appearance of a key entity → pop/fade
- UI/screenshot → zoom/track
- transformation or before/after → morph
- key phrase → mask_reveal/highlight
- list progression → slide_up/stagger
- emotional story → minimal fade/slow push-in

Avoid:
- multiple competing motions at once
- bounce/shake on serious material
- motion that starts before the spoken cue
